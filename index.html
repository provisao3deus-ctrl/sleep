Does your day go a bit like this?

  1. 3:45pm

    The afternoon slump hits, so you reach for another coffee.

  2. 10:30pm

    You’re exhausted, but your mind won’t switch off.

  3. 3:07am

    Awake again, running through tomorrow’s list.

  4. 6:45am

    The alarm goes off, and yesterday’s workout is still in your legs.

This is really common for women in their 30s, 40s and 50s. Work, stress, training and hormone shifts can all play a part. It doesn’t mean something is wrong with you. Often your evenings just need a little more support.

3 things you can try tonight

No new gear and no big overhaul. Pick one, try it for a week, and see how you feel.

  1. After lunch

    Set a caffeine cutoff

    Research suggests even coffee six hours before bed may cut into your sleep. Try stopping about eight hours before bedtime. Energy drinks, pre-workout and some teas count too.

  2. 1 hour before bed

    Dim the lights and dial down screens

    Bright evening light can tell your brain it’s still daytime. Switch to lamps, lower your screen brightness, and charge your phone outside the bedroom if you can.

  3. In bed

    Breathe in for 4, out for 6

    Slow breathing with a longer exhale may help your body shift into a calmer state. Lights off, phone out of reach, ten slow rounds.

Questions

Do I need a supplement for this to work?

No. All seven habits are free and need nothing new. The supplement is an optional extra some women choose, and only you and your doctor can decide if it fits you.

How fast will I notice a difference?

It varies. Pick one habit and give it a week or two before adding the next. Small, steady changes are easier to keep.

When should I talk to a doctor?

If poor sleep has lasted for weeks, gets in the way of your day, or comes with symptoms that worry you, that’s a good reason to check in with your doctor.
