Better sleep and recovery after 30 begin with your evening.

Hormones, stress and busy days can all change how you sleep. A few calm habits may help you wind down, rest well and wake up with more energy.

3 things you can try tonight

No new gear and no big overhaul. Pick one, try it for a week, and see how you feel.

  1. With dinner

    Add a few magnesium-rich foods

    Surveys suggest many US adults get less magnesium from food than recommended. Easy sources: pumpkin seeds, spinach, almonds and black beans.

    Toss pumpkin seeds on a salad, stir spinach into pasta or soup, or build a bowl around black beans. Almonds make an easy evening snack.

  2. 1–2 hours before bed

    Dim the lights and dial down screens

    Bright light late in the evening can make it harder to feel sleepy. Switch to lamps, lower your screen brightness, and if you can, charge your phone outside the bedroom.

  3. The last 30 minutes

    Wind down the same way each night

    A short routine you repeat every night can become a signal that the day is done. Keep it simple: a few minutes of gentle stretching, a warm shower, a few pages of a paper book. Same order, low lights.
